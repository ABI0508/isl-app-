ISL Emergency Communicator

Prototype. The output is a communication request, not a medical diagnosis. Nothing is sent without your confirmation. Recognises 5 signs: help, doctor, pain, call, accident.

Emergency contact (saved only on this device)

Sit about 1.5 m from the camera with both hands, head and shoulders visible. Use a well-lit room. Landscape orientation works best on phones.
